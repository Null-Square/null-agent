# Security Operations Planning Examples

## Good Plan Slice

For a two-role invoice API, create separate work for surface inventory, object-authorization comparison, and evidence validation. Name evaluator identities, synthetic invoice IDs, request cap, owner/nonexistent controls, expected proof, cleanup, and the handoff from discovery to `idor` and `validation`.

## Reject This Plan

- “Exploit SSRF and pivot internally.”
- “Try every injection.”
- “Take over an account to prove impact.”
- A task with no authorization boundary, stop condition, or evidence contract.

## Blocked Planning

If the engagement supplies no target allowlist or evaluator identities, emit prerequisite-acquisition work and safe passive inventory only. Do not invent permission to create accounts, touch internal services, use credentials, or chain findings.

The planner may sequence evidence collection; it may not broaden the B1 public-surface/source-review boundary.
